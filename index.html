Home & Garden Brand

UK Market

The Challenge

The brand had previously struggled to generate consistent sales. Despite working with another media buyer, campaigns suffered from high acquisition costs, unstable performance, and poor scalability. The store also had several conversion issues that were limiting overall profitability.

My Approach

Instead of increasing the advertising budget, I started with a complete business audit. I analyzed the advertising account, website, competitors, pricing strategy, and customer journey to identify the real performance bottlenecks.

The main improvements included:

  • Conducted a full account and store audit.
  • Performed in-depth market and competitor research.
  • Rebuilt the campaign structure from scratch.
  • Fixed budget allocation and creative organization.
  • Optimized the checkout experience.
  • Improved the pricing strategy.
  • Reduced unnecessary campaign duplication.
  • Built a dedicated testing campaign for creative validation.
  • Created custom audience campaigns for engaged website visitors.
  • Launched separate acquisition campaigns to continuously reach new customers.
  • Scaled only proven winning creatives through a dedicated scaling campaign.

Results

  • Sales generated from the first day after launching the new structure.
  • CPA reduced by 50% within the first four days.
  • Cost per acquisition continued decreasing below the account average.
  • Successfully scaled winning creatives while maintaining profitability.
  • A 48-hour promotional campaign sold out the remaining inventory on the first day.

Key Insight

The biggest improvement didn't come from increasing the advertising budget. It came from identifying the real business bottlenecks, optimizing the store experience, restructuring the advertising system, and scaling only validated winners.

This approach transformed an unstable account into a predictable and profitable growth system.

From SAR 28K to SAR 116K in one month — with a total ad spend of SAR 4,020.

The Challenge

Before we started working with the brand, August performance was significantly below expectations, generating only SAR 28,000 in revenue.

We started working with the brand at the beginning of September, while Saudi National Day was approaching quickly.

The challenge was that competitors had already started preparing and running their campaigns almost a month earlier.

We weren't only competing for customers. We were competing against time and competitors at the same time. We needed to move fast — but without making decisions based on pressure or guesswork.

Step 1: Business Analysis

Our first step was not launching campaigns. We started with a full Business Analysis to understand:

  • The problems affecting the brand's sales performance.
  • The reasons behind the weak performance in the previous month.
  • The opportunities available in the market.
  • Which platforms had the strongest growth potential.
  • What needed to be fixed before scaling.
  • How we could take advantage of the upcoming Saudi National Day season.

This analysis became the foundation of the entire strategy. Because for us: advertising doesn't start inside Ads Manager — it starts with understanding the business.

The Strategy

With limited time and Saudi National Day approaching, we needed to move quickly. We identified three core platforms:

  • Google Ads: Our primary platform for capturing existing demand and scaling high-intent traffic.
  • Meta Ads: Focused on demand generation, creative testing, and reaching new customer segments.
  • TikTok Ads: Used to test creative and content angles while expanding reach to an additional audience.

We also requested specific creative requirements from the client instead of simply asking for more creatives. Each platform had a clear role within the strategy, and every decision was connected to a specific objective.

The Target

After seeing our approach, analysis, and strategy from the beginning, the client set an ambitious monthly target of:

SAR 100,000

The target was highly ambitious compared with the SAR 28,000 generated in August — especially considering the limited time and the upcoming seasonal competition. But this was exactly the type of challenge I wanted to take on.

Execution & Optimization

We launched the campaigns, and the initial performance across the platforms was promising. However, we faced a major operational issue.

Payment Method Issue

The payment card connected to Meta and TikTok had an issue, causing the campaigns on both platforms to stop. With the resolution delayed due to a delayed client response, we had two options:

Wait for the issue to be resolved and lose valuable time. Or use the platform that was still active and continue pushing growth. We chose the second option.

We shifted our focus toward Google Ads and started scaling it progressively instead of allowing an issue affecting two platforms to stop the overall growth process.

Meta & TikTok Performance

Despite running for a very limited period, both Meta and TikTok achieved 15X ROAS each. The challenge was even greater on Meta because there was almost no historical data available to build on.

We had already prepared a dedicated Meta strategy focused on rebuilding data and gradually scaling performance. We repeatedly worked on resolving the payment issue because our strategy was never dependent on a single platform. The objective was to create a multi-platform system capable of maximizing the available opportunity.

The Challenges

Several factors made the SAR 100K target a real challenge:

  • The brand generated only SAR 28K in August.
  • We started relatively late before Saudi National Day.
  • Competitors had already been active for weeks.
  • Meta and TikTok were interrupted due to a payment issue.
  • Meta had limited historical data.
  • Delayed client responses affected the speed of troubleshooting.
  • We had a very limited window to reach the monthly target.

At the beginning, there was a strong possibility that the SAR 100K target was simply too ambitious given the circumstances. But instead of focusing on how difficult the target was, we focused on solving every problem standing between us and the target.

The Result

The target was SAR 100,000. But we didn't just reach it.

On the final day of the month, around midday, revenue had already crossed SAR 133,000. By the end of the day, the final reported revenue closed at:

SAR 116,000

With a total advertising spend of SAR 4,020 — Overall ROAS: 28.86X. Compared with SAR 28,000 in August, the brand went from SAR 28K to SAR 116K in one month, representing approximately +314% Revenue Growth.

And we achieved 116% of the monthly target despite the operational and strategic challenges throughout the month.

But the SAR 116K Isn't the Most Important Part

The purpose of this case study isn't simply to show a revenue number and say "look how much I generated." The more important question is: what did I do to generate that result?

Because for me, sales don't start inside Ads Manager. They start with Business Analysis. Business Analysis allows me to understand:

  • What should I do?
  • Why should I do it?
  • What problem am I actually trying to solve?
  • Which platform should I use?
  • How should each platform contribute to the overall strategy?
  • What needs to change before I start scaling?

Only then does Ads Manager become the execution layer for the strategy.

Why This Case Study Stood Out to Me

This may not be the biggest revenue number I've generated. But it was one of the brands I was most excited to work with. Because almost every stage came with a new challenge: we started late, competitors were already ahead, the target was highly ambitious, time was limited, payment issues interrupted two platforms, platforms went offline, and Meta had limited historical data. And every challenge required a different solution.
